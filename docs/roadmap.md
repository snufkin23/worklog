# Roadmap

## Goal
A small, finished, deployed tool that I use daily to track work and generate
standup-ready updates.

## Phases
- [ ] **Phase 1:** database, API, CLI, deployed, CI
- [ ] **Phase 2:** digest job (rule-based summary and tomorrow's plan)
- [ ] **Phase 3:** Flutter app and morning push notification
- [ ] **Phase 4:** tests, docs, demo video

## Later (only after the above works)
- Weekly rollup from stored digests
- LLM-written summaries (privacy-reviewed first)
- Integrations such as Jira or Slack

## Deliberately out of scope
- Multi-user accounts and login
- Microservices
- Anything that stores confidential company data
